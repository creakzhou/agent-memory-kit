## Context

One week of running the three-layer setup. What held and what did not.

## Body

Held up:
- Grepping the index before reading anything saved real time on day two onward.
- The pointer rule caught three rules that were duplicated in two layers.
- Writing a review note daily took four minutes and paid for itself by Thursday.

Did not hold up:
- Recall was manual every time. Nothing was automatic, because no hook existed.
- Two notes drifted into each other and stated different numbers for the same
  measurement. Neither had a date, so neither could be trusted.

## Decision / conclusion

Add a date and a `History` table to every note that contains a number. Undated
facts are opinions wearing a lab coat.
