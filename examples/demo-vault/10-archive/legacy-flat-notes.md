## Context

The pre-migration folder. Kept so old decisions stay findable, not because anyone
should read it end to end.

## Body

40 files, no structure, three near-duplicate meeting logs. Everything of value
was moved into numbered folders during [[vault-migration]]. What remains here is a
record of what the old shape looked like, plus a few dead ends worth not
repeating.

## Decision / conclusion

Do not delete an archive to feel tidy. Deleting is how a system loses the ability
to explain its own past mistakes.
